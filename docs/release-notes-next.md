## Umbuchungen moves to the menu

The Transfers screen that arrived in 2.9.0 was put in the navigation bar at
the bottom, which made five destinations where there had been four. It fits,
but it is cramped — "Umbuchungen" next to "Statistiken" leaves neither much
room on a normal phone.

It lives in the menu now, under General, directly beneath Buchungen. Nothing
else about it changes, and nothing is harder to reach: it opens the same way
Konten, Zahlungsempfänger and Tags always have. The bar goes back to
Dashboard, Buchungen, Budgets and Statistiken.
