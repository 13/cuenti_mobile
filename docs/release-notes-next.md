## The category charts answer a tap

Tapping a slice of the income or expense chart opens the categories beneath
it — but until it opened, nothing on screen said the tap had landed. On a
slow tap it felt like nothing had happened.

The slice under your finger now swells, the way the overview chart's slices
always did. A slice with nothing beneath it swells too, so a tap that opens
nothing still shows you which slice you hit; tapping it again puts it back.

The donut on the overview and the ones on the income and expense tabs were
built separately and did not agree on their measurements — the size of the
hole in the middle, the gap between slices, how far a touched slice grows.
They match now, with the legend centred beneath each.
